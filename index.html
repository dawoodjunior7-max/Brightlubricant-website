About the Company

A company built on verifiable quality.

Bright Lubricants was founded on a straightforward conviction: the lubricant market deserves products backed by real documentation — ASTM test data, OEM approvals, and a traceable supply chain — not just marketing claims.

We source our lubricants from Ukrainian manufacturing facilities operating to European standards. Every batch is tested before dispatch. Every shipment carries a Certificate of Analysis. That is our baseline — not our selling point.

What We Stand For

Three commitments.
No exceptions.

🔍
Source Integrity

Every product we distribute originates from verified Ukrainian manufacturing facilities. We are transparent about where our products are made, how they are tested, and what approvals they carry. We do not misrepresent origin.

📋
Documentation First

A Certificate of Analysis is not optional — it is our standard. Every batch, every shipment. MSDS sheets, technical data sheets, and customs documentation are prepared as a matter of course, not on special request.

Long-Term Partnership
